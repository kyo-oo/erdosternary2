\section*{Non-circular perfect-power block collision}

Fix a GST graph energy $E$, a horizontal width $M$, a vertical block length $L$, and a starting ternary level $q$.  Write
\[
D_t(p):=\operatorname{graphPhaseRowDensity}(E,t,p)
\]
and
\[
W_t(q,L):=\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}3^{q+j}D_t(q+j)
=\operatorname{graphPhaseWindow}(E,M,L,q,t).
\]

The already-formalized horizontal runner identity gives, at every ternary row $p$,
\[
5D_M(p)=4^M D_0(p).
\tag{1}
\]
This is an exact identity, not an inequality and not an asymptotic statement.
Multiplying (1) by the positive weight $3^p$ and summing over the block
$p=q,q+1,\ldots,q+L-1$ yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
5W_M(q,L)
&=\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}3^{q+j}\,5D_M(q+j)\\
&=\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}3^{q+j}\,4^M D_0(q+j)\\
&=4^M W_0(q,L).
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
Thus the complete vertical phase window is transported horizontally with the exact positive scale factor $4^M/5$.

Now assume a child Happy cell occurs at the left boundary at level $q$.  The local 83-density/phase-density positivity theorem implies
\[
D_0(q)>0.
\]
Every weight $3^{q+j}$ is positive, while all other local densities are nonnegative whenever needed by the already-formalized canonical phase-window theorem.  Consequently
\[
W_0(q,L)>0.
\tag{3}
\]

Assume simultaneously that every cell on the right boundary throughout the same vertical block is bad.  The formalized bad-cell density theorem gives
\[
D_M(p)\le 0
\qquad(q\le p<q+L),
\]
and therefore
\[
W_M(q,L)\le0.
\tag{4}
\]

But $4^M>0$ and $5>0$.  From (3),
\[
4^M W_0(q,L)>0,
\]
whereas from (4),
\[
5W_M(q,L)\le0.
\]
This contradicts the exact transport identity (2).  Hence the hypotheses
\[
\text{left child Happy}\quad\text{and}\quad\text{right boundary all bad}
\]
cannot coexist.

Therefore the perfect-power block collision is non-circular and follows directly from the existing rowwise horizontal conservation law:
\[
\boxed{
\bigl(\exists p\;\mathrm{Happy}(E,0,p)\bigr)
\;\wedge\;
\bigl(\forall p\;\neg\mathrm{Happy}(E,M,p)\bigr)
\Longrightarrow\bot.
}
\]

The Lean implementation should mirror exactly these four steps:
\begin{enumerate}
\item obtain the child Happy level $q$;
\item use \texttt{gpt56\_canonical\_phase\_window\_positive\_of\_happy} for $W_0>0$;
\item use \texttt{gpt56\_canonical\_phase\_window\_nonpositive\_of\_all\_bad} for $W_M\le0$;
\item sum \texttt{gpt56\_horizontal\_phase\_row\_exact} over the block to obtain $5W_M=4^M W_0$, then close by arithmetic.
\end{enumerate}
